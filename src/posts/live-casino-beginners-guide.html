What makes it "live"?

Each table is hosted by a dealer and streamed in real time. You place your bets on screen, the dealer deals the cards or spins the wheel, and you see the result just like you would at the table.

Popular live games

Blackjack

Aim for a hand closer to 21 than the dealer's, without going over. You decide whether to take another card ("hit") or keep your hand ("stand").

Roulette

Bet on where the ball will land – a single number, a colour, odd or even, or a group of numbers – then watch the dealer spin the wheel.

Baccarat

Choose whether the Player hand, the Banker hand or a tie will come out on top. The dealer follows fixed rules, so it's easy to pick up.

Tip: watch a few rounds before you play. It's the easiest way to learn the pace of a table and how betting works.
