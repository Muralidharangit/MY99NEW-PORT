Sports betting adds an extra layer of excitement to the games you already love. If you're just getting started, these basics will help you place your first bet with confidence.

How odds work

Odds show how likely an outcome is thought to be – and what you'll get back if it happens. With decimal odds, your potential return is simply your stake multiplied by the odds. For example, a stake of ₹100 at odds of 2.50 would return ₹250 if the bet wins.

Lower odds mean an outcome is seen as more likely; higher odds mean it's seen as less likely.

Common bet types

  • Match result – back a team to win, or the match to end in a draw where that's possible.
  • Totals – bet on whether the score will be over or under a set number.
  • Multiples – combine several selections in one bet. Every selection must win.

Check before you confirm: review your bet slip – the selection, the odds and your stake – before placing a bet.

Habits that keep it fun

  • Bet on sports you know and follow.
  • Set a budget for the week and stick to it.
  • Don't bet to win back losses.
  • Remember that results are never guaranteed.
