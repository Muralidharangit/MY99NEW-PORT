Aviator is a fast, simple game that's easy to learn. Each round, a plane takes off and a multiplier climbs with it. Your job is to decide when to cash out – before the plane flies away.

How a round works

  1. Place your bet before the round starts.
  2. Watch the plane take off. The multiplier rises as it climbs.
  3. Cash out at any point to collect your bet times the current multiplier.
  4. If the plane flies away before you cash out, that bet is lost.

Every round is random

There's no pattern to when the plane will fly away, and past rounds don't predict the next one. Treat each round on its own and play for entertainment.

Decide your limits first. Pick a budget and a point at which you'll stop, before you start playing – and stick to them.

Tips for playing sensibly

  • Start with small bets while you learn the pace of the game.
  • Rounds are quick, so take regular breaks.
  • Never chase losses by raising your bets.
