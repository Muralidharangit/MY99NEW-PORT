Your account holds your funds and your winnings, so keeping it secure matters. These six habits take minutes and make a real difference.

1. Use a strong, unique password

Choose a long password you don't use anywhere else. A short phrase with numbers and symbols is easier to remember and harder to guess.

2. Keep your OTP and UPI PIN private

One-time passwords and your UPI PIN are for you alone. MY99EXCH will never ask for them – not by phone, chat or email.

If someone asks for your OTP or PIN, it's a scam. Don't share it, end the conversation and let our support team know.

3. Complete account verification

We may ask you to verify your identity (KYC). It helps protect your account and makes sure withdrawals reach the right person.

4. Watch out for fake links

Only sign in through the official MY99EXCH website. Be careful with links in messages that ask you to "confirm" or "unlock" your account.

5. Sign out on shared devices

Always sign out when you use a phone or computer that isn't yours.

6. Tell us if anything looks wrong

Notice a login or payment you don't recognise? Contact our support team straight away – we're available 24/7.
