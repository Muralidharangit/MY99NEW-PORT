UPI is one of the easiest ways to pay in India, and it's how you deposit and withdraw on MY99EXCH. This guide walks you through both – plus a few simple checks that keep your money safe.

Making a deposit

  1. Choose UPI as your payment method when you deposit.
  2. Check the details – confirm the amount and the UPI ID before you continue.
  3. Approve the payment in your UPI app.

Once the payment is confirmed, the funds appear in your MY99EXCH account and you're ready to play.

Withdrawing your winnings

  1. Choose UPI when you request a withdrawal.
  2. Enter your UPI ID carefully – double-check every character.
  3. Confirm the request. We may ask you to verify your identity (KYC) to protect your account.

Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone – not even someone who says they're from support. MY99EXCH will never ask for them.

Checking a payment

Want to know where a deposit or withdrawal is up to? Contact our support team with your registered username and the amount and date of the payment, and they'll check its latest status for you.

Quick checklist

  • Use a UPI ID in your own name.
  • Check the amount before approving.
  • Keep your UPI app and phone up to date.
  • Reach out to support if anything looks unusual.
